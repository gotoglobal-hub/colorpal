# Mission 18: Project-Based Palettes

Your users are working on multiple hobby projects at once and are getting their paint lists mixed up.

Let's help them out by allowing them to create "Projects". A project will contain a specific palette of paints from their main collection. This will let them easily see all the colors they need for their "Orc Warlord" or their "Ocean Diorama" without having to search through their entire collection.

**Goal:** Create a UI to define projects and add/remove paints to them.
