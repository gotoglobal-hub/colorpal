# Mission 19: Color Theory Helper

Not everyone is a master artist with an innate sense of color harmony. Let's give your users a helping hand.

When a user selects a color, this feature will suggest other paints from their collection that are complementary, analogous, or part of a triadic color scheme. This will help them build beautiful and harmonious color palettes for their projects.

**Goal:** Implement a "Color Harmony" suggestion panel that appears when a user views a paint's details.
