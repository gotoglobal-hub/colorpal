# Mission 20: Community Palettes

Your users are proud of the palettes they've created and want to share them with the world!

This mission is to build a "Community Palettes" section where users can publish their projects. Other users can then browse, import, and rate these shared palettes. This will create a vibrant community and provide tons of inspiration.

**Goal:** Add functionality to publish a project/palette and a public gallery to view them.
